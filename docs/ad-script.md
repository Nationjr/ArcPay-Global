# ArcPay Global: "Pay Like You're Home" (Ad Script)

**Runtime:** about 75 seconds (a 30 second cutdown is at the end)
**Tone:** warm, cinematic, lightly funny, no hard sell
**Setting:** one busy global city (think Dubai, Toronto or London). The three leads are a traveler, a resident and a student, so they can all end up at the same store. Establish this with a skyline shot at the start.
**Music:** a soft afrobeat groove with light percussion that builds in Scene 4 and drops to a single piano note at the black screen.

---

## Characters

- **TUNDE (late 20s, Nigerian):** sharp, well dressed, relaxed, a little proud. He wants a luxury perfume.
- **DANIEL (40s, Canadian):** plaid jacket, a bit lost with local things, friendly. He drives a dusty SUV with a rattle.
- **ANANYA (early 20s, Indian):** a student with a backpack and earbuds. She is hungry and practical.
- **MECHANIC (50s):** big laugh, oil-stained hands, a phone full of unread messages.
- **CASHIER (30s):** calm, friendly, proud of the ArcPay Global stand on the counter.

---

## SCENE 1: THE PERFUME HUNT (0:00 to 0:14)

**EXT. SKYLINE, MORNING.** Fast aerial of the city. Traffic, towers, a plaza with a sparkling fountain.

**EXT. LUXURY PLAZA, DAY.** TUNDE steps out of a taxi, adjusts his cuffs and checks his phone. We see a wallet app with a healthy USDC balance. He smiles and pockets the phone.

**INT. PERFUME BOUTIQUE #1.** Gleaming shelves. He picks up a bottle, sniffs and nods. At the counter he points at a card machine and asks:

> **TUNDE:** Do you take USDC?
> **CLERK:** (polite, confused) USD... cash? Or card?
> **TUNDE:** No, USDC. From my wallet.
> **CLERK:** (shakes head) Sorry, sir. Card only.

**INT. PERFUME BOUTIQUE #2.** A shop with a gold sign. He tries again and gets an apologetic shrug and a pointed finger at the ATM outside.

**EXT. PLAZA STEPS.** Tunde stands with his hands on his hips and looks at his phone. The money is right there. It just has nowhere to go.

> **TUNDE (to himself):** Na wa o. The money is there, but where do I spend it?

*Camera pulls back as he starts walking, still searching.*

---

## SCENE 2: THE WORKSHOP (0:14 to 0:24)

**EXT. AUTO WORKSHOP, DAY.** A dusty SUV rolls in with a loud rattle. DANIEL steps out, rubbing the back of his neck.

**INT. WORKSHOP.** The MECHANIC wipes his hands on a rag and leans under the hood.

> **DANIEL:** It makes this sound... (does a terrible rattle impression)
> **MECHANIC:** (laughing) I can hear it from the street. Leave it with me. Come back this evening.
> **DANIEL:** Perfect. Thanks, man.

Daniel hands over the keys, glances at the street and leaves on foot. The mechanic is already under the car. *(Keep this scene short and light. It sets up the payoff later.)*

---

## SCENE 3: THE HUNGRY STUDENT (0:24 to 0:36)

**EXT. FOOD STREET, MIDDAY.** Steam, spices and neon signs. ANANYA walks with earbuds in, scrolling her phone.

**INT. RESTAURANT #1.** She reads the menu and spots a small card-reader sticker at the register. She pulls out her phone and shows the wallet screen.

> **ANANYA:** I don't want to swap my money or use my bank card. Can I pay in USDC?
> **OWNER:** (kind but blank) Only cards and cash, miss.

**INT. GROCERY STORE #2.** She checks the shelves, then the payment signs. Nothing. She sighs and her stomach growls loudly. A passer-by looks at her. She laughs, embarrassed.

> **ANANYA (muttering):** Somebody please accept my money.

*Camera follows her out the door as she turns the corner.*

---

## SCENE 4: THE STORE (0:36 to 1:05)

**EXT. A SMALL CONVENIENCE AND GIFT STORE.** Warm light. In the window, a clean sign reads **"ArcPay Global: Available here."**

*Tunde passes the window, stops and does a double take. He steps back and reads it. His face lights up.*

**INT. STORE.** Shelves of snacks, gifts, a small premium perfume corner. Tunde walks to it and picks a bottle of the perfume he has been hunting for. He takes it to the counter.

At the counter, ANANYA is already paying. She has a bag of food. The CASHIER types her total into a phone on a stand. A **QR code** appears. Ananya scans it with her wallet, taps once and the screen flashes **"Payment Received."**

> **ANANYA:** (grinning, to herself) Finally.

*Tunde watches, impressed. He mouths: "Wow."*

**CUT TO: INT. WORKSHOP.** The mechanic's phone buzzes. He wipes his hands, types and sends: **"Your car is ready."** *(Show the message on screen.)*

**CUT TO: EXT. STREET.** Daniel gets the message, looks at his phone and breaks into a jog. He goes back toward the workshop.

**INT. WORKSHOP.** Daniel gets his keys. The SUV starts, purring instead of rattling. The mechanic holds up a phone with a QR on screen.

> **MECHANIC:** (proud) We take ArcPay Global now.
> **DANIEL:** (laughs) Even for car repairs?

Daniel scans, pays and the success screen flashes. He shakes the mechanic's hand and drives off.

**INT. STORE, BACK TO TUNDE.** It is his turn. The cashier smiles and keys in his amount. Tunde scans the QR with his phone and sends USDC straight from his wallet. No card. No transfers. No stress. The screen glows green. **"Payment Received."**

> **CASHIER:** Thank you, sir. Your receipt is on its way to your email.

*Tunde's phone buzzes with the email receipt. He smiles, takes his perfume and heads for the door.*

---

## SCENE 5: THE CROSSING (1:05 to 1:15)

**EXT. STORE ENTRANCE, GOLDEN HOUR.** Tunde steps outside with a small luxury bag. Through the glass window behind him we see **Ananya** at a bus stop, eating from her food bag, looking at her phone for a cab.

*Their eyes meet through the glass for a second. A small, knowing nod. Two strangers who just solved the same problem.*

*Behind them, on the main road, Daniel's SUV glides past with the sun on the windshield. He sees them both, raises a hand and drives on. Three people who never met, now connected by one small thing.*

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.** The music drops to a single piano note.

---

## END CARD (1:15 to 1:20)

**BLACK SCREEN.** The ArcPay Global logo fades in: a white circle with the USDC symbol on a blue background, with the name under it.

> **VO (calm, confident, a little smile in the voice):** ArcPay Global. Spend your USDC. Anywhere.

**TAGLINE ON SCREEN:** *Pay like you're home.*
**URL:** arc-pay-global.vercel.app

## 30 Second Cutdown

1. **(0:00 to 0:06)** Quick cuts of Tunde, Daniel and Ananya each failing to pay in USDC. Same music, quick beat.
2. **(0:06 to 0:12)** All three arrive at the ArcPay Global store sign.
3. **(0:12 to 0:22)** Montage: QR code on a phone, three scans, three "Payment Received" green screens, the email receipt.
4. **(0:22 to 0:30)** Tunde, Ananya and Daniel's car pass each other. Cut to black. Logo and tagline.

---

## Production Notes

- **Keep the app screens real.** Film the actual POS keypad, the QR and the green "Payment Received" screen from arc-pay-global.vercel.app. Record screen captures on a phone and drop them into the shots.
- **Do not say "crypto" in the voiceover.** Say "USDC" and "your wallet". It keeps the tone mainstream.
- **Show the email receipt buzz** at the end of Scene 4. It is a quick moment that proves the full flow.
- **Casting:** the Nigerian lead's accent is a strength. Keep his lines natural and relaxed, slightly playful.
- **Shot variety:** use handheld for the search scenes (1 to 3) and smooth gimbal or slider shots once they reach the store, so the mood visibly calms.
- **Sound design:** sell the "ding" of the success screen. A soft chime on each payment becomes the ad's audio signature.
- **Do not show real card brands** in the failed-payment shots. Use generic card machine props.
